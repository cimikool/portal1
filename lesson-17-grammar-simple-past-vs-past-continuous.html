Grammar: Simple Past vs. Past Continuous
🔬 Science & Technology A1.2 Common Core

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can use the simple past and past continuous together — with when and while — to describe two events that happened at the same time in the past.

📋 The key pattern

Pattern 1: while / when + past continuous + simple past
An action was IN PROGRESS (past continuous) WHEN a shorter action INTERRUPTED it (simple past).

Pattern 2: while + past continuous + past continuous
Two actions were happening at the same time.
Pattern 1
was/were + ingwhensimple past
Pattern 2
while + was/were + ing,was/were + ing

💬 Examples

Pasteur was studying bacteria when he made a great discovery.Long action (studying) + short interruption (made) ✓
While Marie Curie was working in her lab, she discovered polonium.while + past continuous + simple past ✓
The electricity went out while I was watching a science documentary.Simple past (went out) interrupted the past continuous (was watching) ✓
While one scientist was writing the report, another was running the experiment.Two actions happening at the same time ✓
Einstein was living in Germany when World War I started.Background situation + event that happened ✓

💡 When vs. While

WordUsed withExample
whensimple past (short event)… when she discovered radium.
whilepast continuous (ongoing action)While she was working…
Both are correct:
She was working when she discovered radium. ✓
While she was working, she discovered radium. ✓
