Lesson 2 · English · A1.2
Art Vocabulary
A1.2 Common Core Moroccan Curriculum

🎨 Introduction

In this lesson we learn vocabulary related to art. You will be able to talk about art materials, types of art, and describe a painting in English.

This vocabulary is part of the Common Core programme for A1.2 level in Morocco.

🖌️ Art materials

Things you use to make art
🖌️
a paintbrush
noun
A brush used to paint pictures.
"She uses a paintbrush to paint."
🎨
paint
noun / verb
A coloured liquid used to make pictures.
"He puts paint on the canvas."
✏️
a pencil
noun
Used to draw or write.
"I draw with a pencil first."
🖼️
a canvas
noun
A flat surface used for painting.
"The artist paints on a canvas."
📐
a ruler
noun
Used to draw straight lines.
"Use a ruler to draw a straight line."
✂️
scissors
noun (plural)
Used to cut paper or fabric.
"Cut the paper with scissors."

🖼️ Types of art

Different kinds of art
🖼️
a painting
noun
A picture made with paint.
"This is a beautiful painting."
✏️
a drawing
noun
A picture made with a pencil or pen.
"She made a drawing of her house."
🗿
a sculpture
noun
A 3D work of art made from stone, clay, or metal.
"The museum has a marble sculpture."
📸
a photograph
noun
A picture taken with a camera.
"He took a photograph of the city."

🗣️ Describing art

colourful
adjective
Has many bright colours.
beautiful
adjective
Very attractive to look at.
realistic
adjective
Looks like real life.
abstract
adjective
Does not show real objects.
This is a colourful painting of flowers. Describing what you see using an adjective.
The artist is famous because his paintings are very realistic. Using a conjunction + art vocabulary together.
I like abstract art, but my friend prefers realistic paintings. Combining Lesson 1 (but) with art vocabulary.

👨‍🎨 People and places

an artist
noun
A person who makes art.
"She is a talented artist."
a museum
noun
A place where art is displayed.
"We visited a museum in Rabat."
an exhibition
noun
A public display of art.
"The exhibition opens next week."
a gallery
noun
A room or building for showing art.
"The painting is in the gallery."

💡 Remember

When you describe art in English, use: This is a/an + adjective + type of art

✓ "This is a beautiful painting."
✓ "This is a colourful drawing."
✓ "This is an abstract sculpture."

You can also combine with because and but from Lesson 1 to give your opinion!
