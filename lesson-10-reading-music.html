🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can read a text about a musician, identify True / False statements, and use evidence from the text to explain your answers — using all the grammar and vocabulary from Unit 7.

You read a biography in Lesson 6 and practised comprehension questions. Now you will read a second text — this time focused on music — and practise the True / False reading task, which is very common in Moroccan English exams.

📖 Before you read

Key vocabulary — learn these words before reading:

reggae = a music style from Jamaica
poverty = being very poor
protest = to speak against injustice
spiritual = related to the soul / religion
influence = to have an effect on others
release = to publish / put out a song or album
struggle = a difficult fight for something
generation = people born at the same time
True / False strategy: For each statement, find the relevant sentence in the text. If the text says the same thing → True. If the text says something different or the opposite → False. Never answer from memory — always go back to the text.

📄 Read the text

Bob Marley — The Voice of a Generation
A biography of the king of reggae music
Paragraph 1 — Early life

Robert Nesta Marley, known as Bob Marley, was born on 6 February 1945 in the small village of Nine Mile, in the parish of Saint Ann, Jamaica. His father was a white British man and his mother was a young Jamaican woman. Bob grew up in poverty, but he found comfort and joy in music from a very early age.

Paragraph 2 — Music career

As a teenager, Bob moved to Kingston, the capital of Jamaica, where he began his music career. He formed a band called The Wailers in 1963. Together, they developed a new style of music called reggae — a slow, rhythmic music with powerful messages about life, love, and justice. Bob wrote songs about poverty, equality, and the struggles of ordinary people.

Paragraph 3 — World fame

By the 1970s, Bob Marley became famous all over the world. His album Exodus, released in 1977, was one of the most important albums of the 20th century. His music was not only about entertainment — it was also spiritual and political. He used his songs to protest against injustice and to give hope to millions of people.

Paragraph 4 — Legacy

Bob Marley passed away on 11 May 1981, at the age of 36. Despite his short life, his influence on world music was enormous. He sold more than 75 million records worldwide, making him one of the best-selling music artists of all time. Today, his songs still inspire new generations of musicians and listeners all over the world.

Grammar spotlight: Count the irregular past verbs in the text — grew, found, began, formed, wrote, became, sold. You learned all of these in Lesson 7!

✏️ True / False practice

Read each statement carefully. Go back to the text to find the answer.

True False

True False
