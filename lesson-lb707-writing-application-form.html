🎯 Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can complete an application form correctly — using appropriate formal language, accurate personal information, and well-structured supporting statements.

📋 What is an application form?

An application form is a formal document used to apply for something — a programme, a job, a scholarship, a volunteer position, or a community project. It requires:

Personal details: Full name, date of birth, address, contact details.
Education / experience: School, qualifications, relevant activities.
Personal statement / motivation: Why you are applying, what you can contribute.
Declaration: A statement confirming the information is true.

📄 Model application — Youth Citizenship Programme

APPLICATION FORM — Youth Citizenship Programme
Please complete all sections in BLOCK CAPITALS or clearly printed English.
Section 1 — Personal Details

Full name: BENALI NADIA    Date of birth: 15/03/2006
Address: 12, Rue Al Qods, Fès, Morocco
Email: nadia.benali@email.com    Phone: +212 6XX XXX XXX

Section 2 — Education

Current school: Abdellah Guennoun High School, Fès
Year / Level: 2nd Baccalaureate — Sciences Physiques
Languages: Arabic (native), French (fluent), English (B1+)

Section 3 — Motivation Statement

I am applying for the Youth Citizenship Programme because I strongly believe that young people have a vital role to play in building a better society. As a student, I have participated in several community activities, including a neighbourhood clean-up campaign and a literacy drive for younger students.

I believe that good citizenship requires both knowing your rights and fulfilling your responsibilities. If accepted, I would contribute by organising awareness workshops on civic rights for students at my school. As my teacher advised me, "You ought to use your voice for the common good."

Section 4 — Declaration

I confirm that the information provided above is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge.
Signature: Nadia Benali    Date: 20/03/2025

Notice: The motivation statement uses vocabulary from L701 (rights, responsibilities, common good), advice language from L703 (ought to), and reported speech from L704 (quoted the teacher). All Unit 7 content comes together here.

🛠️ Useful language for motivation statements

Opening: I am applying for… because… / I wish to apply for… in order to…
Experience: I have participated in… / I have experience of… / I have been involved in…
Skills: I am able to… / I have the ability to… / I am committed to…
Contribution: If accepted, I would… / I intend to contribute by… / My goal is to…
Closing: I look forward to the opportunity to… / I am confident that…
