Vocabulary: Cultural Values
🌍 Cultural Values 2nd Baccalaureate

🎯 Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can use key vocabulary about cultural values, identify opposites, and talk about cultural concepts in English at B1+ level.

🌍 Core vocabulary

🤝
tolerance
noun
Accepting and respecting beliefs, habits, or customs different from your own.
💛
altruism
noun
Caring about others before yourself; selfless concern for the wellbeing of others.
🌐
coexistence
noun
Living peacefully alongside others who have different beliefs or ways of life.
⚖️
equity
noun
Fairness and justice in the way people are treated, especially in society.
🌈
diversity
noun
The existence of a variety of cultures, ethnicities, ideas, or perspectives in a group.
🏛️
citizenship
noun
Being an active, responsible member of a community or country.
💡
initiative
noun
The ability to act independently and make decisions without being asked.
🔖
stereotype
noun
A fixed, oversimplified image or idea about a particular group of people.
🤲
cooperation
noun
Working together with others towards a shared goal.
🎭
culture
noun
The customs, arts, beliefs, and values shared by a group of people.

🔄 Opposites — key pairs

Positive valueNegative opposite
toleranceintolerance
responsibilityirresponsibility
cooperationrivalry / conflict
altruismselfishness / egoism
respectdisrespect
coexistenceconflict / segregation
Word formation tip: Many opposites use prefixes:
in- → intolerance, inequality, irresponsibility
dis- → disrespect, disagreement, dishonesty

💬 Using the vocabulary in context

Tolerance is the foundation of a peaceful multicultural society.Key sentence for essays on cultural values.
We should fight against stereotypes because they prevent us from seeing people as individuals.Critical thinking on culture — exam-level argument.
Diversity enriches a society by bringing together different ideas, talents, and perspectives.Positive argument for diversity — useful for writing tasks.
Active citizenship means participating in community life, not just following the law.Extended definition of citizenship.
Coexistence between different cultures requires mutual respect and open dialogue.Linking coexistence to respect and dialogue.
