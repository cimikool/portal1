Reading: An Artist's Biography

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can read a biography and find key information — name, nationality, dates, and achievements — using the reading skills and grammar from Lessons 3, 4, and 5.

You already know how to ask biography questions (Lesson 5) and use was / were (Lesson 4). Now you will practise reading a biography and finding answers to those same questions in a text.

📖 Before you read — reading strategy

How to read a biography efficiently:
  1. Skim the text quickly — read only the first sentence of each paragraph to get the main idea.
  2. Look for names, dates, and places — they are usually the answers to biography questions.
  3. Find key verbs — was, were, studied, painted, performed — they tell you what the person did.
  4. Answer the questions — go back to the text and find the exact line.

Key vocabulary in the text below — make sure you know these words:

gifted = very talented
to study = to learn
to perform = to sing/act live
audience = people watching
to pass away = to die (polite)
legend = a very famous person
emotion = a feeling
century = 100 years

📄 Read the biography

Umm Kulthum — The Star of the East
A biography of one of the greatest Arab artists of all time
Paragraph 1 — Who was she?

Umm Kulthum was one of the most famous singers in the Arab world. She was born in a small village in Egypt in 1904. Her father was a religious leader and a lover of music. From a very young age, Umm Kulthum was a gifted singer with a powerful and emotional voice.

Paragraph 2 — Early life

As a child, she often performed with her father at local celebrations. The audiences were always amazed by her voice. When she was a teenager, her family moved to Cairo, the capital of Egypt. Cairo was the centre of music and culture in the Arab world. There, she studied music and quickly became very popular.

Paragraph 3 — Career and fame

By the 1930s, Umm Kulthum was the most famous singer in Egypt. Her concerts were legendary — they sometimes lasted for five or six hours! Her voice was full of deep emotion, and her songs were about love, longing, and the beauty of the Arabic language. Millions of people listened to her on the radio every month.

Paragraph 4 — Legacy

Umm Kulthum passed away in 1975 in Cairo. She was 70 years old. More than four million people attended her funeral — one of the largest in history. Today, she is still called "Kawkab al-Sharq" — the Star of the East. She was, and still is, a true legend of Arab music.

Notice: The highlighted words are all forms of was / were — the grammar you learned in Lesson 4. Can you count how many times they appear in the text?

🔍 Language focus — was / were in the text

Go back to the biography and find all the examples of was and were. Here are the most important ones:

was born · was a gifted singer · was the most famous · was full of emotion · was 70 years old · were amazed · were legendary · were about love
