Unit 8 is all about Science and Technology. Scientists study the world around us and make discoveries that change our lives. Let's start with the key vocabulary.

🔬 Scientists and their fields

🧪
a scientist
noun
A person who studies science and does experiments.
⚗️
a chemist
noun
A scientist who studies chemistry — atoms, molecules, and reactions.
⚡
a physicist
noun
A scientist who studies physics — energy, motion, and matter.
🔢
a mathematician
noun
An expert in mathematics — numbers, equations, and logic.
🏥
a doctor / physician
noun
A person who studies medicine and treats sick people.
💡
an inventor
noun
A person who creates new machines or devices.

🏭 Science vocabulary

🔭
a laboratory
noun
A room where scientists do experiments. Short form: lab.
🧫
an experiment
noun
A scientific test to find out something new.
💉
a vaccine
noun
A medicine that protects you from a disease.
🦠
a disease
noun
An illness that affects the body or mind.
⚙️
an invention
noun
A new machine, device, or process created by an inventor.
💻
technology
noun
The use of science to create useful machines and systems.
Word families to know:
science → scientist → scientific
invent → inventor → invention
discover → discoverer → discovery

🌍 Famous scientists

Marie Curie was a physicist and chemist. She discovered two chemical elements: polonium and radium.Polish-French scientist — first woman to win a Nobel Prize.
Louis Pasteur was a French scientist. He invented the process of pasteurisation and developed vaccines against diseases.His work saved millions of lives.
Albert Einstein was a German-American physicist. He is famous for his theory of relativity: E = mc².He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921.
Ibn al-Haytham was an Arab scientist and mathematician. He is called the father of modern optics — the science of light.He was born in Basra, Iraq, around 965 AD.
