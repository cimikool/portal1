Grammar: Reported Speech

🎯 Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can use reported speech to report statements, questions, and advice — with correct tense backshift and pronoun changes.

📋 Reported statements — tense backshift

Direct
"I respect the law."
Reported
He said (that) he respected the law.
Direct speech tenseReported speech tense
present simple → "I vote"past simple → he said he voted
present continuous → "I am voting"past continuous → she said she was voting
past simple → "I voted"past perfect → he said he had voted
will → "I will apply"would → she said she would apply
can → "I can help"could → he said he could help
Key words that also change:
now → then / at that time  |  today → that day  |  tomorrow → the next day
here → there  |  this → that  |  I → he/she  |  my → his/her

❓ Reported questions

Yes/No Q
"Do you vote?"→He asked if / whether she voted.
Wh- Q
"What are your rights?"→She asked what their rights were.
Important: In reported questions, the word order is NOT inverted — it follows normal statement order.
✓ He asked what she thought.    ✗ He asked what did she think.

📣 Reported functions — advice and commands

Command / instruction:
Direct: "Apply for the programme." → Reported: She told me to apply for the programme.told + object + to-infinitive ✓
Advice:
Direct: "You should vote." → Reported: He advised me to vote.advised + object + to-infinitive ✓
Request:
Direct: "Please respect the law." → Reported: She asked me to respect the law.asked + object + to-infinitive ✓
Prohibition:
Direct: "Don't litter!" → Reported: He told me not to litter.told + object + not to + infinitive ✓

💬 Citizenship examples

The teacher said, "Every citizen has rights and duties." → The teacher said that every citizen had rights and duties.present simple → past simple ✓
The mayor said, "We will build a new community centre." → The mayor said that they would build a new community centre.will → would ✓
She asked, "Do young people vote in elections?" → She asked whether young people voted in elections.yes/no question → whether + statement order ✓
