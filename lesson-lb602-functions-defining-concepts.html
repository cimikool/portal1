💬 Examples — cultural values

Tolerance can be defined as the willingness to accept beliefs and customs different from one's own.Pattern 1 — most formal ✓
Altruism refers to the practice of putting others' needs before your own.Pattern 2 ✓
A stereotype is a fixed idea about a group of people that is often unfair or inaccurate.Pattern 5 — category word + relative clause ✓
Coexistence means living peacefully with others despite having different cultural or religious backgrounds.Pattern 3 — simple and clear ✓
Citizenship is used to describe the active participation of individuals in the social and political life of their community.Pattern 4 — formal / academic ✓
Exam tip: In the 2BAC oral and writing exam, you are often asked to "define" a concept. Always use one of these patterns — never just say "it is when people…"

💡 Structuring a complete definition

A complete definition has three parts:

1. The term + defining pattern + 2. Category word + 3. Distinguishing featureTolerance can be defined as [a value] [that involves accepting others' beliefs and customs].
Common category words: a value, a concept, a quality, a process, a practice, a behaviour, an attitude, a principle, a term
