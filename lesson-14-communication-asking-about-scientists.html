Communication: Asking About Scientists
🔬 Science & Technology A1.2 Common Core

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can ask and answer questions about scientists — their nationality, field of work, discoveries, and dates — using was/were, question tags, and the simple past.

You learned vocabulary about scientists (L012) and question tags (L013). Now you put it together in real communication — asking for and giving information about scientists.

❓ Key questions about scientists

Who was Marie Curie?→ She was a Polish-French physicist and chemist.
What did she discover?→ She discovered radium and polonium.
When did she win the Nobel Prize?→ She won it in 1903 and again in 1911.
Where was she born?→ She was born in Warsaw, Poland.
What was she famous for?→ She was famous for her research on radioactivity.
She was a scientist, wasn't she?→ Yes, she was. (question tag for confirmation)

💬 Model dialogue — asking about Pasteur

Ayman and Fatima complete a biography task in class.

Ayman
Who was Louis Pasteur?
Fatima
He was a French scientist and chemist.
Ayman
What did he discover?
Fatima
He discovered that bacteria cause disease. He also invented a process called pasteurisation.
Ayman
He developed vaccines too, didn't he?
Fatima
Yes, he did. He developed vaccines against rabies and anthrax.
Ayman
When did he live?
Fatima
He was born in 1822 and died in 1895.
Notice: Ayman uses a question tag — "He developed vaccines too, didn't he?" — to check the information. This sounds natural and polite in English conversation.
