Communication: Biography Questions

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can ask and answer questions about a person's life — who they were, where they were born, what they were famous for — using was / were from Lesson 4.

In Lesson 4 you learned was and were. Now you will use them in real conversations about artists. These are the exact questions Moroccan students use in biography tasks and exams.

❓ The 6 biography questions

Who was he / she?
He / She was a painter / singer / actor…
→ Asks about the person's identity or profession.
When was he / she born?
He / She was born in 1881 / in the 19th century.
→ Asks about the date or year of birth.
Where was he / she born?
He / She was born in Spain / Egypt / England…
→ Asks about the country or city of birth.
What was he / she famous for?
He / She was famous for his / her paintings / songs / films…
→ Asks about their greatest achievement.
Where was he / she from?
He / She was from Spain / Egypt / England…
→ Asks about nationality or origin. Similar to "Where was he born?"
When did he / she die?
He / She died in 1973 / 1977…
→ Uses simple past — we will practise this more in Lesson 7.

💡 Key tip: Question word order

In biography questions, the question word (Who / When / Where / What) comes first, then was / were, then the subject.

Where + was + he + born? ✓
NOT: Where he was born? ✗
Where was Picasso born? — He was born in Spain. Question word + was + subject ✓
What was Umm Kulthum famous for? — She was famous for her powerful voice. Question word + was + subject ✓
Who was Charlie Chaplin? — He was a British actor and director. Question word + was + subject ✓
When was Leonardo da Vinci born? — He was born in 1452. Question word + was + subject ✓

💬 Model dialogue — talking about Picasso

Read this conversation between two students, Salma and Youssef.

Salma
Who was Picasso?
Youssef
He was a famous Spanish painter and sculptor.
Salma
Where was he born?
Youssef
He was born in Málaga, Spain, in 1881.
Salma
What was he famous for?
Youssef
He was famous for his colourful and abstract paintings. His most famous work was Guernica.
Salma
When did he die?
Youssef
He died in 1973. He was 91 years old.
