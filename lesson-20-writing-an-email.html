📄 Model email

To: teacher@school.ma
Subject: My Science Project — Marie Curie

Dear Mr Sahmoudi,

I am writing to tell you about my science project. I chose to write about Marie Curie because she was an amazing scientist.

Marie Curie was a Polish-French physicist and chemist. She discovered two chemical elements: radium and polonium. She also won the Nobel Prize twice — in 1903 and in 1911. While she was working in her laboratory, she developed her theory of radioactivity.

I found this project very interesting and inspiring. I would like to know if I can add more information about her inventions.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Salma Benali

Notice: The email uses vocabulary from L012 (scientist, chemist, elements), grammar from L015 (simple past), and L017 (while she was working). All unit knowledge comes together here!

🛠️ Useful language for emails

Opening: I am writing to ask / tell / inform you about…
Adding information: I would also like to… / In addition,…
Asking: Could you please… / I would like to know if…
Closing: I look forward to your reply. / Thank you for your time.
Sign-off: Yours sincerely, / Best regards, / Kind regards,
