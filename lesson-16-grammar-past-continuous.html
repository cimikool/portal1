Grammar: Past Continuous
🔬 Science & Technology A1.2 Common Core

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can use the past continuous (was/were + verb-ing) to describe actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

You already know the simple past from Unit 7 (did, went, discovered). Now you will learn the past continuous — a different way to talk about the past.

⚙️ How to form the past continuous

✅ Positive
Subject+was / were+verb + ing
❌ Negative
Subject+wasn't / weren't+verb + ing
❓ Question
Was / Were+subject+verb + ing?
SubjectPositiveNegative
I / He / She / Itwas workingwasn't working
You / We / Theywere workingweren't working
When to use it: Use the past continuous for an action that was in progress (happening, not finished) at a particular moment in the past.

Time expressions: at 8 o'clock, when…, while…, at that moment, all morning

💬 Examples — scientists at work

Marie Curie was working in her laboratory when she made the discovery.Action in progress (working) → past continuous ✓
The scientists were conducting an experiment at 9 o'clock this morning.Action in progress at a specific time ✓
Pasteur was studying bacteria when he discovered the vaccine.was + studying (in progress) ✓
I was watching a documentary about Einstein last night.Action in progress last night ✓
They weren't sleeping — they were still working at midnight.Negative + positive past continuous ✓
Was she reading about radioactivity? — Yes, she was.Question + short answer ✓

💡 Simple past vs. Past continuous — quick comparison

She discovered radium. (simple past — completed action)The discovery happened and finished.
She was working in the lab. (past continuous — ongoing action)The work was in progress — not focused on when it finished.

You will learn how to use them TOGETHER in the next lesson.
