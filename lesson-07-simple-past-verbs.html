Grammar: Simple Past — Regular & Irregular Verbs
🎨 The Arts A1.2 Common Core

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can use the simple past tense with regular verbs (painted, studied, performed) and irregular verbs (went, wrote, sang) to talk about what artists did in the past.

In Lesson 4 you learned was / were — the past of "to be". Now you will learn the past of action verbs — the verbs that describe what people did. This is the main grammar tool for writing biographies.

⚙️ How to form the simple past

✅ Positive
Subject + verb + ed / irregular form
❌ Negative
Subject + didn't + base verb
❓ Question
Did + subject + base verb ?
Key rule: In negatives and questions, always use the base form of the verb after didn't / did.
✓ He didn't paint  ·  ✓ Did she sing?  ·  ✗ Did she sang?

📗 Regular verbs — add -ed

Most verbs are regular — just add -ed to make the past form. But watch the spelling rules!

Most verbs → + ed
paint → painted
perform → performed
Verb ends in -e → + d
compose → composed
create → created
Short vowel + consonant → double + ed
stop → stopped
plan → planned
Verb ends in -y → -ied
study → studied
try → tried

💬 Examples — artists in action

RegularPicasso painted his masterpiece Guernica in 1937. paint + ed = painted ✓
RegularUmm Kulthum studied music in Cairo as a young woman. study → studied (y → ied) ✓
RegularCharlie Chaplin performed in silent films in the 1920s. perform + ed = performed ✓
IrregularMozart wrote his first symphony when he was only eight years old. write → wrote ✓ (irregular)
IrregularUmm Kulthum sang for millions of people across the Arab world. sing → sang ✓ (irregular)
IrregularCharlie Chaplin won an honorary Oscar in 1972. win → won ✓ (irregular)
NegativePicasso didn't study in a normal school — he had a private teacher. didn't + base form (study, NOT studied) ✓
QuestionDid Chaplin make colour films? — No, he didn't. Did + subject + base form ✓

❓ Questions with did

To ask a question in the simple past, use Did at the start. Always use the base form of the verb — never the past form.

Biography questions with Did
Did he paint portraits? — Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
Where did she study music? — She studied in Cairo.
What did he write? — He wrote songs and symphonies.
When did she become famous? — She became famous in the 1930s.
