Communication: Inventions & Inventors
🔬 Science & Technology A1.2 Common Core

🎯 What you will learn

By the end of this lesson, you can ask and answer questions about inventions and inventors — who invented something, when, where, and why it is important.

❓ Questions about inventions

Who invented the telephone?→ Alexander Graham Bell invented the telephone in 1876.
When did he invent it?→ He invented it in 1876.
Where did he make this invention?→ He made it in the United States.
What is it used for?→ It is used to communicate over long distances.
How did this invention change the world?→ It allowed people to talk to each other from far away.

💬 Model dialogue — talking about inventions

Karim
Who invented the light bulb?
Nour

Karim
What is it used for?
Nour
It is used for lighting homes, streets, and buildings. Before the light bulb, people used candles.
Karim
It changed the world completely, didn't it?
Nour
Yes, it did! It was one of the most important inventions in history.
