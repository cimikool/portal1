📐 The biography structure — 4 paragraphs

A good biography at A1.2 level has four clear paragraphs. You saw this structure in both Umm Kulthum's biography (L006) and Bob Marley's biography (L010). Now you will use it yourself.

Paragraph 1
🌟 Introduction — Who was this person?
Name · nationality · profession · birth date and place
Ask yourself: Who was he/she? When and where was he/she born?
Paragraph 2
🎒 Early life & education
Family background · where he/she grew up · how he/she started in the arts
Ask yourself: Where did he/she grow up? How did he/she start?
Paragraph 3
🏆 Career & achievements
Main works · awards · what he/she became famous for
Ask yourself: What did he/she create? What was he/she famous for?
Paragraph 4
🌍 Legacy
When he/she died · why he/she is still remembered · his/her influence today
Ask yourself: When did he/she die? Why do we still remember him/her?

🛠️ Useful language for each paragraph

Paragraph 1 — Introduction
… was born in … in … (place, year)
… was a famous … (nationality + profession)
… was one of the greatest …
Paragraph 2 — Early life
As a child / As a teenager, …
… grew up in …
… showed great talent for … from an early age
… studied … at / in …
Paragraph 3 — Career
By the 1960s / 1970s, … became famous for …
… wrote / painted / composed / sang …
His / Her most famous work was …
… won an award for …
Paragraph 4 — Legacy
… passed away in … at the age of …
… is still remembered today for …
His / Her influence on … was enormous
… inspired generations of …

📄 Model biography — Leonardo da Vinci

Read this model. The amber words are was/were (L004). The green words are action verbs (L007).

Leonardo da Vinci
Italian painter, inventor, and scientist (1452–1519)
Paragraph 1 — Introduction

Leonardo da Vinci was one of the greatest artists and thinkers in history. He was born on 15 April 1452 in the small town of Vinci, in Tuscany, Italy. He was a painter, a sculptor, an inventor, and a scientist — a true genius of the Renaissance.

Paragraph 2 — Early life

As a child, Leonardo showed great talent for drawing and observation. At the age of fourteen, he went to Florence to study painting with the famous artist Verrocchio. He learned quickly and soon became more talented than his teacher.

Paragraph 3 — Career & achievements

Leonardo painted some of the most famous works in art history. His most famous painting was the Mona Lisa — a portrait of a woman with a mysterious smile. He also painted The Last Supper, a masterpiece of religious art. Leonardo did not only paint — he also designed flying machines, bridges, and weapons that were centuries ahead of his time.

Paragraph 4 — Legacy

Leonardo da Vinci passed away on 2 May 1519 in France, at the age of 67. His influence on art, science, and architecture was enormous. Today, the Mona Lisa is still the most visited painting in the world. Leonardo was, and still is, a symbol of human creativity and imagination.

What makes this biography good?
✓ Four clear paragraphs · ✓ All verbs in simple past · ✓ Specific dates and places · ✓ Vocabulary from Unit 7 (portrait, creativity, imagination, masterpiece) · ✓ A strong final sentence

✅ Before you submit — writing checklist

Use this checklist every time you write a biography.

📋I have 4 paragraphs: introduction, early life, career, legacy.
⏪All main verbs are in the simple past (was, wrote, painted, became…).
📅I used at least two dates or time expressions (was born in… / by the 1970s…).
🌍I mentioned the person's nationality and profession in paragraph 1.
🏆I mentioned at least one famous work or achievement in paragraph 3.
🎨I used at least two Unit 7 vocabulary words (painting, composer, inspiring, creative…).
💡My final sentence says why this person is still remembered or important.
