📄 Read the text

Bill Gates and the Computer Revolution
How one person changed the way we use technology
Paragraph 1 — Early life

William Henry Gates III, known as Bill Gates, was born on 28 October 1955 in Seattle, USA. As a teenager, he became passionate about computers. He was writing computer programmes when he was only 13 years old.

Paragraph 2 — Microsoft

In 1975, Gates dropped out of Harvard University to found Microsoft with his friend Paul Allen. Microsoft developed the MS-DOS operating system, which became the standard software for personal computers worldwide. By the 1990s, Microsoft's Windows operating system was on almost every computer in the world.

Paragraph 3 — Impact

Bill Gates made computers accessible to ordinary people. Before Microsoft, computers were used only by scientists and large companies. Gates believed that every home and every office should have a personal computer. His vision changed the world of technology forever.

Paragraph 4 — Philanthropy

In 2000, Gates left his role as CEO of Microsoft and focused on charity work. He and his wife Melinda founded the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, which works to fight disease and poverty around the world. Today, Gates is one of the most generous philanthropists in history.

Grammar spotlight: Paragraph 1 uses the past continuous — "He was writing computer programmes when he was only 13." This is the grammar from Lessons 16 and 17!
