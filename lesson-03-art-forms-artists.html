Unit 7 is all about The Arts. The arts include everything humans create to express beauty, emotion, and ideas — paintings, songs, films, sculptures, and more.

🖼️ Three main art forms

Painting & Visual Arts
🖌️
a painting
noun
A picture made with paint on canvas or paper.
🗿
a sculpture
noun
A 3D work of art made from stone, metal, or clay.
👤
a portrait
noun
A painting or photo of a person's face.
🖼️
a canvas
noun
The flat surface an artist paints on.
Music
🎵
a song
noun
A short piece of music with words.
🎻
an orchestra
noun
A large group of musicians playing together.
🎼
a composition
noun
A piece of music written by a composer.
🎤
a performance
noun
When an artist performs live in front of people.
Cinema & Theatre
🎬
a film / a movie
noun
A story told through moving pictures and sound.
🎭
a role
noun
The character an actor plays in a film or play.
🏆
an award
noun
A prize given to an artist for excellent work.
📽️
a scene
noun
One part of a film or play that happens in one place.

👩‍🎨 People in the arts

The person who does the art has a special name.

🖌️ painter / artist
A person who paints pictures. — "Picasso was a famous Spanish painter."
🗿 sculptor
A person who makes sculptures. — "Rodin was a French sculptor."
🎼 composer
A person who writes music. — "Mozart was a great composer."
🎤 singer / songwriter
A person who sings or writes songs. — "Umm Kulthum was a legendary Egyptian singer."
🎭 actor / actress
A person who plays a role in a film or play. — "Charlie Chaplin was a brilliant actor."
🎬 director
A person who makes and controls a film. — "The director told the actors what to do."

💡 Key tip: Art form → Person → Verb

painting → painter → to paint
music → composer / singer → to compose / to sing
cinema → actor / director → to act / to direct
sculpture → sculptor → to sculpt

When you learn a new art word, always try to learn the person and the verb too. This helps you speak and write better!

🌍 Famous artists — do you know them?

Pablo Picasso (1881–1973)
A Spanish painter and sculptor. Famous for his colourful, abstract paintings. Born in Málaga, Spain.
Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519)
An Italian painter and inventor. Famous for the Mona Lisa — one of the most famous portraits in history.
Umm Kulthum (1904–1975)
An Egyptian singer and songwriter. She was the most famous Arab singer of the 20th century. Her performances lasted for hours.
Charlie Chaplin (1889–1977)
A British actor and director. Famous for silent films. He also wrote music for his movies.
